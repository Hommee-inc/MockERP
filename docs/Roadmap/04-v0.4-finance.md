# v0.4 — Finance

## Goal

Model the financial side of a confirmed sale: an account receivable, one or
more payments, and refunds caused by returns.

## Rules

- a confirmed sale creates an account receivable;
- a payment belongs to one account and may be partial;
- a return creates a refund against the related account;
- payments and refunds cannot exceed the remaining balance;
- financial values are derived from sales and returns, not regenerated;
- dates follow the business event order.

## Validation

Test partial payments, multiple payments, partial returns, and a sale with no
payment yet. A generator that allows a payment or refund to exceed the
remaining amount is producing a bug, not a controlled anomaly.

## Portfolio angle

“Connected sales to receivables, payments, and refunds while preserving the
remaining balance across partial events.”
