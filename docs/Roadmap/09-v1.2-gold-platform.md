# v1.2 — Databricks Gold

Build business-facing tables and reconciliation metrics from Silver.

## Planned outputs

- revenue by day and customer;
- product performance;
- outstanding receivables and payments;
- inventory position and movement history;
- one reconciliation view across budget, sale, inventory, and finance.

Take care with one-to-many joins. Aggregate each source at the right grain
before combining them, otherwise a sale with several items or payments can be
counted more than once.

The reconciliation table should classify records as `MATCH` or `MISMATCH` with
a documented rounding tolerance.
